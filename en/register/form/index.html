Does the child have any health limitations?

For any medical problem or limitation we will bring a note from a specialist, and we will inform the school of any change in our child's health.

Drop-off and pickup

I understand that bringing the children in the morning and picking them up at the end of the day is solely the parents' responsibility.

If an adult not on the list picks up the child, we will notify the administration in advance, and the child will be released only with ID.

Photo permission
Do you authorize photographing the child at school and using the photos for internal purposes or social media?
