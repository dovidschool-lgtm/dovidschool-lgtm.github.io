Admissions

Join the Dovid School family

The 2026-2027 school year began on August 17, 2026. Want to join during the year? Write to us and we'll work out the details.

Tuition and registration

Annual tuition

72,000 pesos

Split into 10 monthly payments of 7,200 pesos.

Registration fee

1,000 pesos

In cash or by bank transfer. Not part of tuition.

Meals, school

500 pesos a month

Separate sign-up for breakfasts and lunches. At the preschool, meals are included in the day.

  • Payment is due on the 28 of each month, in pesos only. Paying in dollars carries an additional fee.
  • Enrollment is not possible without a tuition payment agreement.

How to enroll

  1. Fill out the form

    Includes the health declaration and authorization of who drops off and picks up the child. One form per child.

  2. Send documents

    A copy of the CURP, ID or passport of the child and both parents, and a photo of the child.

  3. Pay the registration fee

    And sign the enrollment policies.

  4. Agree on tuition

    And set the start date together.

Enrollment form

Enrollment is completed right here, from your phone or computer. The details are stored only in the school's account.

Cancellations and payments

  • The registration fee is refunded only if the child was not accepted by decision of the administration. If the parents cancel, it is not refunded.
  • A child's withdrawal must be announced before the 5 of the month, and that month is paid in full. If notice is given after the 5, an additional month is also charged.
  • Absences for any reason, such as illness or travel, do not exempt from tuition payments.
