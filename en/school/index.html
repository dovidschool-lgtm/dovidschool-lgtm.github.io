School

A school day at Dovid School

A day that begins with prayer, continues with six lessons and structured breaks, and ends at 3:00 pm.

Schedule

  1. 8:15-8:30Arrival
  2. 8:30-9:15Prayer
  3. 9:15-10:00First lesson
  4. 10:00-10:15Breakfast
  5. 10:15-10:30Recess
  6. 10:30-11:15Second lesson
  7. 11:15-12:00Third lesson
  8. 12:00-12:15Recess
  9. 12:15-13:00Fourth lesson
  10. 13:00-13:15Lunch
  11. 13:15-13:30Recess
  12. 13:30-14:15Fifth lesson
  13. 14:15-15:00Sixth lesson

The gate opens at 8:15 and lessons begin at 8:30.

What matters to us

Languages

Classes are taught in Hebrew and Spanish, plus English lessons.

Respect for everyone

Respecting the dignity of every person, young or old, is a core value of our school community.

Continuous learning

Hands-on, continuous and complete learning processes are a foundation of our work.

A screen-free day

No phones or electronic devices are brought to school, so the day stays free for learning and being together.
