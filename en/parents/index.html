School rules

Respect for others

Respecting the dignity of every person, young or old, is a core value of our community. We do not accept violence or the use of force of any kind; such cases are handled by the teachers, the administration and the parents.

We use clean and respectful language, speak politely to the staff, and follow instructions in class, at recess and during activities.

Caring for the building

Damaging school equipment means repairing the damage or covering its cost.

At the end of the day we leave the classroom clean, chairs up and the board erased.

Dress

Students may choose their clothing, as long as it is respectful and appropriate: modest dress, clean and neat clothes, without slogans, prints, pictures or loud colors, and long hair tied back.

Footwear

Students wear closed shoes or sandals with a back strap. Flip-flops, Crocs and slippers are not allowed, and for safety shoes stay on at school.

Food

Students bring two generous meals each day: breakfast and lunch. At 12:00 there is a fruit break, provided by the school.

Anyone who wishes can sign up separately for the breakfasts and lunches served at the preschool, for 500 pesos a month.

Ball games

Soccer and dodgeball are allowed only in coordination with the teachers, on agreed days and recesses. Other ball games are played only in the yard, never in classrooms or inside the building.

Phones and electronic devices

Phones are not to be brought to school. In special cases, a request is filled out at the office and written permission is given; the phone stays in the office until the end of the day.

Cameras, music players, smartwatches and electronic games are not allowed. An unauthorized device is held and returned only to the parents. The school is not responsible for damage, loss or theft.

Arrival, lateness and early pickup

The gate opens at 8:15 and lessons begin at 8:30. For safety, please do not stay on the premises longer than needed for drop-off and pickup.

Early pickup is possible only with advance notice to the teacher and the administration.

Absences

Please plan family vacations around the school calendar. If a child needs to miss more than 3 days, notify the administration in writing. Catching up is the responsibility of the student and parents.

In case of illness, let the teacher know as soon as possible. The student returns when healthy and no longer contagious, and the parents coordinate with the staff how to make up the material.

Lice prevention

Parents check their children's hair regularly. A student with lice or nits goes home and returns after full treatment and confirmation from the parents. The school may carry out checks when needed.

Office phone

The school phone is for work and emergencies only. Visits to friends are arranged in advance.

Lost and found

The school is not responsible for lost items. It is best not to bring valuables. Clothing that is found goes into the lost-and-found box.

Birthdays

Birthday celebrations follow the same format for all students. A store-bought cake may be brought, only from bakeries on the island with a recognized kosher certificate and a kashrut seal. The teacher sets aside an hour for a festive activity, and we recommend bringing tzedakah coins for the whole class.

Preschool policies

Drop-off and pickup

At drop-off and pickup, a staff member waits at the entrance, receives your child and hands them back to you in person. The gate is open until 9:15 and pickup begins at 14:45.

For safety, please do not stay longer than needed. Visitors enter only with permission from the administration and by appointment.

Health and safety

With fever, diarrhea, vomiting or a contagious illness, the child stays home for at least 24 hours after symptoms end. If symptoms appear during the day, the staff will call you to pick up your child as soon as possible.

Check regularly for lice and nits. If any are found, we will ask you to pick up your child for treatment at home, and we will gladly welcome them back after a full cleaning.

Children wear closed, non-slip shoes. For safety, slippers, Crocs and flip-flops are not allowed.

Food and nutrition

Breakfast and lunch are served at the preschool, so please do not send food, sweets or snacks.

Please note: the preschool has peanut and egg allergies. Let us know about any sensitivity or allergy.

Birthdays

We celebrate every child during their birthday month, in coordination with the teacher. Cake and refreshments only from the island's kosher bakeries, with a kashrut seal.

Personal items

A backpack with 2 changes of clothes, diapers and wipes if needed, a personal water bottle, and a pacifier or comfort object if needed. Everything labeled with the child's name.

Sleep and rest

After lunch there is an hour of rest. Please send a personal sheet and blanket, labeled with the child's name.

Potty training

The process is done together by the parents and the staff, according to each child's readiness. We recommend starting at preschool after a successful week at home with very few accidents, and in coordination with the staff. During this stage, please send several changes of clothes each day.

Early literacy: what helps at home

Reading and writing are built from early childhood, above all through enjoyment. A few simple ideas for the daily routine:

Read together every day

About 15 to 20 minutes of shared reading, with questions and conversation during the story.

Play with letters

Look for objects that start with a letter, make up rhymes and use memory cards.

Create and write

Draw and tell stories, and shape letters from play dough, sand or flour.

Make it part of the day

Read signs at the supermarket, play word games in the car and write the shopping list together.

Milestones by age

Ages 2 to 3

  • Hold a book the right way up
  • Show interest in stories and pictures
  • Recognize familiar symbols and shapes

Ages 3 to 4

  • Understand that writing stands for words and meaning
  • Recognize some letters, especially those in their name
  • Play with rhymes and sounds

Ages 4 to 5

  • Recognize most letters
  • Write their name and basic letters
  • Understand the direction of reading and writing

Ages 5 to 6

  • Recognize all the letters and their sounds
  • Try writing on their own
  • Are ready for first reading
