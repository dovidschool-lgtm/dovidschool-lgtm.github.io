Our vision

Where heart and mission meet

In the heart of Cozumel, a new generation of children is growing up with a sparkle in their eyes and a mission in their hearts.

Here every day begins with a smile, a hug and the trust that a child who feels loved, safe and seen can go as far as possible.

Our vision is to raise shining souls: children who know their roots, proud of their Jewish identity, connected to Torah, their people and their values, and above all, who believe in themselves and in their ability to do good in the world.

We combine academic excellence with a deep Chassidic education, a way of life built on love of others, truth, joy and holiness. Our goal is not only to impart knowledge, but to shape soul, heart and character. To teach not only how to learn, but how to live: with honesty, perseverance, responsibility and love.

A professional Jewish and Israeli teaching team guides every child on their personal journey, with plenty of listening, sensitivity and trust.

Our story

The school was founded by Menachem Mendel and Sarah Chanzin, in the name of their grandfather, Rabbi Dovid Chanzin, and opened its doors in Cozumel in February 2019.

Rabbi Dovid Chanzin

5671 - 19 Tammuz 5763

Rabbi Dovid Chanzin founded the Chabad school network in Israel, Oholei Yosef Yitzchak Lubavitch, and led it for twenty-five years.

His devotion to Jewish education knew no bounds. He carried out the Rebbe's instructions on the education of Jewish children with great dedication, and was the senior rabbi of the Chabad rabbis in the Holy Land.

Following the call of the Lubavitcher Rebbe to "do all you can" to bring Moshiach, he worked tirelessly to spread the message of the Redemption and prepare the world to receive it.
