Challah and welcoming Shabbat

Every Friday the children knead dough together, say the blessing and bake fragrant challahs. Then we welcome Shabbat with songs, candles, wine and challah.

The first days of preschool

While settling in, every feeling is welcome and every difficulty gets a response. You can rest easy: we are with them every step of the way.

  1. Day one9:00-11:00

    Parents stay with their child for an hour or two.

  2. Day two8:30-11:30

    Parents stay 15 to 30 minutes and say goodbye with a hug and a kiss.

  3. Day three8:30-13:30

    Includes lunch. Parents say goodbye at the entrance.

  4. From day fourFull day

    A regular day. Returning children settle in as they need.

How to help with goodbyes

  • Create a short, fixed routine: hang up the backpack, say hi to a favorite toy, and say goodbye.
  • Keep goodbyes short and confident. A brief goodbye makes things easier for your child.
  • Always say goodbye, even if your child is already playing. Don't slip away without a goodbye.
  • Say goodbye together with the teacher, so she is close by as a safe figure.
  • Let the staff know about any change at home, such as a move or a new sibling.
